Now build the STRATEGY / REGIME / CONFLUENCE layer on top of JARVIS's
market-state feature engine.

Do NOT simply create:

indicator bullish = +5
indicator bearish = -5

The objective is to recognize specific trading setups and determine whether
the current market regime is appropriate for them.

Support:

CRYPTO
STOCKS
ETFs
FOREX
FUTURES
COMMODITIES
INDICES

The same indicator must be allowed to mean different things under different
regimes and strategies.

Example:

RSI 75

Strong trending market:
potential momentum confirmation

Range:
potential mean-reversion condition

Short squeeze:
may indicate accelerating forced buying

Therefore:

INDICATORS ARE FEATURES.

STRATEGIES INTERPRET FEATURES.

  "flow": "BUY_DOMINANT"

==================================================
3. STRATEGY FAMILY: TREND CONTINUATION
==================================================

Ideal environment:

established trend
healthy structure
reasonable volatility
supportive volume/flow

Features:

EMA structure
ADX/DMI
HH/HL or LH/LL
Supertrend
relative strength
CVD
RVOL
VWAP/AVWAP

LONG:

uptrend
HH/HL
positive flow
pullback holds structure

SHORT:

downtrend
LH/LL
negative flow
rally fails structure

Avoid chasing extreme ATR extensions.

==================================================
4. BREAKOUT
==================================================

Detect:

Donchian breakout
BOS
range breakout
prior high/low break
volume-profile level break

Confirmation:

RVOL
CVD
OI where applicable
relative strength
volatility expansion
book liquidity

Distinguish:

REAL BREAKOUT
WEAK BREAKOUT
FAILED BREAKOUT

A breakout without participation should receive lower expectancy.

==================================================
5. BREAKOUT RETEST
==================================================

Sequence:

break
expansion
pullback
retest
hold
continuation

Look for:

former resistance -> support

or:

former support -> resistance

Confirmation:

CVD
volume
VWAP/AVWAP
structure
ATR

This should be treated separately from chasing the initial breakout.

==================================================
6. FAILED BREAKOUT / FAILED BREAKDOWN
==================================================

Detect:

price breaks major level
liquidity is swept
price returns inside range
flow fails to confirm
CHoCH occurs

This can produce high-quality reversal setups.

Require confirmation.

Do not fade every breakout.

==================================================
7. LIQUIDITY SWEEP REVERSAL
==================================================

Detect:

previous high/low
swing high/low
session high/low
equal highs/lows
liquidation cluster

Price sweeps level.

Then require:

rejection
reclaim
CVD reversal or divergence
absorption
CHoCH

Potential strategy:

SWEEP -> RECLAIM -> STRUCTURE CHANGE -> ENTRY

==================================================
8. MEAN REVERSION
==================================================

Only activate primarily during appropriate range/mean-reverting regimes.

Features:

VWAP deviation
AVWAP deviation
ATR extension
Bollinger position
volume profile
RSI/MFI
CVD exhaustion
support/resistance

Avoid automatically shorting overbought markets in strong trends.

==================================================
9. SQUEEZE EXPANSION
==================================================

Detect compression using:

BB/Keltner squeeze
ATR percentile
realized-volatility contraction

Then detect expansion:

range break
volume expansion
CVD
structure
relative strength

Squeeze itself has NO direction.

Direction comes from breakout/flow.

==================================================
10. MOMENTUM IGNITION
==================================================

Designed especially for shorter timeframes.

Look for simultaneous acceleration:

ROC
MACD histogram acceleration
RVOL
CVD
order-book imbalance persistence
spread stability
volatility expansion

Require sufficient liquidity.

Avoid entries where expected slippage consumes the edge.

==================================================
11. VWAP / AVWAP RECLAIM
==================================================

LONG example:

price below VWAP
reclaims VWAP
holds above
flow improves
structure turns bullish

SHORT = inverse.

Anchor quality matters.

A major swing AVWAP may be more meaningful than arbitrary rolling VWAP.

==================================================
12. RELATIVE-STRENGTH BREAKOUT
==================================================

Stocks:

asset outperforming:

SPY
sector
relevant index

Crypto:

asset outperforming:

BTC
ETH
relevant crypto basket

Prefer breakouts accompanied by improving relative strength.

Avoid buying nominal breakouts caused purely by broad beta.

==================================================
13. CRYPTO FUNDING / POSITIONING SQUEEZE
==================================================

Crypto-specific.

Potential SHORT SQUEEZE:

extreme negative funding
large short positioning
OI elevated
price refuses to decline
bullish structure break
CVD turns positive

Potential LONG SQUEEZE / LONG LIQUIDATION:

extreme positive funding
large long positioning
OI elevated
price loses structure
CVD turns negative

Funding alone must NEVER trigger the trade.

==================================================
14. CRYPTO LIQUIDATION CASCADE
==================================================

Use:

liquidation clusters
OI
funding
price structure
CVD
volatility

Detect potential forced-move conditions.

Example downside cascade:

support breaks
large long liquidation cluster below
OI elevated
sell CVD accelerates
volatility expands

Do not chase after the liquidation event has already exhausted itself.

==================================================
15. ORDER-FLOW ABSORPTION REVERSAL
==================================================

Where high-quality order-flow data exists:

large aggressive selling
price stops declining
bid liquidity replenishes
CVD continues falling
price holds

Potential seller absorption.

Inverse for buyer absorption.

Require persistence.

One large order-book wall is insufficient.

==================================================
19. MULTI-TIMEFRAME CONFIRMATION
==================================================

Implement explicit timeframe hierarchy.

Example for 5m entry:

1D = structural context
4H = major trend
1H = tactical trend
15m = setup
5m = entry
1m/order book = execution

Do NOT require every timeframe to point identically.

Example:

Daily uptrend
4H pullback
1H stabilization
5m CHoCH upward

can be an excellent trend-continuation entry.

Represent:

higher_timeframe_context
setup_timeframe
execution_timeframe

==================================================
20. SETUP STATE MACHINE
==================================================

Strategies should evolve through states.

Example breakout:

WATCHING
-> APPROACHING
-> BREAKING
-> CONFIRMED
-> RETESTING
-> ENTRY_READY
-> INVALIDATED

Do not generate completely independent signals every candle.

Maintain setup state.

This reduces repeated entries and allows JARVIS to understand market sequences.

==================================================
21. STRATEGY CONFLUENCE
==================================================

Measure independent evidence categories.

Example:

STRUCTURE = bullish
FLOW = bullish
TREND = bullish
VOLUME = bullish
RELATIVE_STRENGTH = bullish
VOLATILITY = supportive

This is better than:

RSI +5
MACD +5
Stochastic +5
CCI +5

because those four may describe the same underlying momentum.

Create category-level confidence.

==================================================
22. CONTRADICTION ENGINE
==================================================

Explicitly identify disagreement.

Example:

Price trend: bullish
Structure: bullish
CVD: bearish divergence
OI: rapidly increasing
Funding: extreme positive
RS: weakening

Do not hide contradictory evidence inside an average.

Expose:

supporting_evidence
contradicting_evidence

Contradiction should affect confidence/EV.

==================================================
23. STRATEGY-SPECIFIC INVALIDATION
==================================================

Every strategy needs deterministic invalidation.

Examples:

breakout:
close back inside range

trend continuation:
break of structural HL/LH

VWAP reclaim:
loss of VWAP + failed reclaim

liquidity sweep:
break beyond sweep extreme

Do not rely solely on arbitrary percentage stops.

==================================================
24. EXPECTED VALUE
==================================================

Every recognized setup should be mapped to historical performance.

Segment by:

strategy
asset class
direction
regime
timeframe

Use hierarchical fallback when samples are small.

Calculate:

P(win)
Avg Win R
Avg Loss R
Gross EV
Expected Costs
Net EV
Sample Size
Confidence Interval

A technically beautiful setup with negative net expectancy:

NO_TRADE.

==================================================
25. COST-AWARE STRATEGY FILTERING
==================================================

Different strategies tolerate different costs.

Example:

5-minute scalp:
spread/slippage extremely important

4-hour swing:
small spread less important

Calculate costs relative to expected move.

Include:

fees
spread
slippage
funding
borrow
market impact

Reject setups whose expected edge is consumed by execution.

==================================================
29. EXPLAINABILITY
==================================================

Every decision should be explainable.

Example:

SOL/USD
Strategy: Breakout Retest
Direction: LONG

Higher timeframe:
4H uptrend

Structure:
15m bullish BOS
5m retest held

Flow:
CVD positive

Derivatives:
OI +4.2%
Funding neutral

Relative strength:
SOL outperforming BTC

Volatility:
expanding

Contradictions:
minor bearish 5m RSI divergence

Historical:
147 comparable trades

Calibrated win probability:
61%

Expected gross R:
+0.34R

Expected costs:
-0.05R

Expected net R:
+0.29R

Decision:
TRADE

The LLM may explain this information.

The LLM must NOT fabricate the underlying measurements.

==================================================
30. TESTING / BACKTESTING
==================================================

Every strategy must be testable independently.

Run:

historical backtest
walk-forward
out-of-sample
shadow mode

Measure:

strategy alone
strategy by asset
strategy by timeframe
strategy by regime

Do not optimize thresholds against the entire dataset.

Prevent lookahead bias.

==================================================
FINAL ARCHITECTURE
==================================================

MARKET DATA
      ↓
FEATURE ENGINE
      ↓
MARKET STATE
      ↓
REGIME ENGINE
      ↓
STRATEGY RECOGNITION
      ↓
CONFLUENCE + CONTRADICTION
      ↓
HISTORICAL CALIBRATION
      ↓
EXPECTED NET R
      ↓
PORTFOLIO RISK
      ↓
TRADE / NO_TRADE
      ↓
EXECUTION ENGINE
      ↓
OUTCOME
      ↓
LEARNING

Do not optimize for the number of signals.

Optimize for:

COST-ADJUSTED EXPECTANCY
DRAWDOWN
RISK-ADJUSTED RETURN
CALIBRATION
ROBUSTNESS

A high-quality NO_TRADE decision is as valuable as finding a trade.
